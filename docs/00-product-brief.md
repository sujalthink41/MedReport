# Baseline — Product Brief

## One-liner
Upload a lab report. Understand what's off, what changed, and what to do about it.

## The problem
A lab report is 40 rows of numbers, a few letters marked `H` or `L`, and no explanation.
The person holding it cannot answer three basic questions:

1. **Is this serious?**
2. **Is it better or worse than last time?**
3. **What do I do now?**

So they either panic-Google their way into cancer forums, or ignore it entirely.

Worse: nobody keeps their old reports. They're scattered across WhatsApp, email, a
physical folder, three different labs, and two cities. So every test is read in
isolation — when the **trend is the actual signal**.

## Who this is for

**Primary — the caretaker.** The 28-year-old managing their parents' health from
another city. A report lands on WhatsApp. They can't read it, can't tell if it's
urgent, and don't want to call the doctor over nothing. Highest emotional intensity,
highest retention.

**Secondary — the self-tracker.** Annual full-body checkup, wants to know if the
numbers are drifting.

## What it does

1. **Upload** — PDF or a phone photo of the report
2. **Read** — extract every parameter, value, unit and reference range
3. **Show** — clean visual view: normal / borderline / out of range / needs attention,
   sorted by what matters, not by the order the lab printed it
4. **Explain** — per marker, in plain language: what it does, why it might be off,
   what symptoms tend to come with it, what typically moves it back
5. **Connect** — panel-level reasoning. Low Hb + low ferritin + low iron is one story
   (iron deficiency anaemia), not three separate flags
6. **Track** — every report stacks on the last. Direction, not just today's number
7. **Prepare** — a one-page sheet for the doctor visit

## The wedge

The **doctor prep sheet**. One page: what changed, what's off, three questions to ask.

This is the shareable moment — the thing people screenshot and forward to family.
It also converts the product from "a viewer" into "a thing that changed my appointment."
Build everything around producing this page well.

## Why this wins

Everyone else stops at *"here's your PDF."*

The value isn't reading one report — it's being the only place that holds **all** of
them, and can say *"this has been climbing for eight months."* That gets more valuable
with every upload and cannot be replicated by a general-purpose chatbot, because the
chatbot doesn't have your history.

## Success metric

**Second upload rate.**

One upload is curiosity. Two is a habit. Nothing else matters in V1.

## Non-negotiable framing

This product helps people **understand**, never **diagnose**.

- "Here's what this marker means and what commonly affects it" ✅
- "You have X, take Y" ❌

This keeps us safe, keeps us honest, and costs nothing in usefulness.
